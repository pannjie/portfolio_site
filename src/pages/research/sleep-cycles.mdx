In 2011, Swiss Engineer Stefan Thomas tried to locate Satoshi Nakamoto by calculating the timing of his forum posts. He discovered that Bitcoin’s inventor was almost completely inactive between the hours of GMT 11pm and 5am.

This absence, interpreted as sleep and used alongside other indicators, suggested that ‘Satoshi Nakamoto’ was *probably* a British man cosplaying as a Japanese man. The Superdry of cryptography.

Out of curiosity (and maybe desperation), I tried this approach on Polymarket by writing a simple script to extrapolate probable timezones from transactions volumes. Here are the inconclusive—but not uninteresting—results:

Baseline. [Domer/ImJustKen](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/who-is-making-bets-on-polymarket-60-minutes/) is a top Polymarket trader known for his geopolitical takes. He is dormant between UTC 8 - 12 am. If we assume that 12 - 6 am is the normal bedtime, this gives us an offset of -8. UTC-8 is the US West Coast—not too far off from Domer’s home in [Las Vegas, UTC-7](https://www.dlnews.com/articles/markets/meet-the-most-prolific-bettor-on-polymarket/).

However, there are other gaps in his schedule which you can’t really explain. Does he drive out to Applebees for dinner around 8pm? Does he break for lunch around 2?

Verdict: *6/10.*

Far-right financier George Cottrell is Nigel Farage’s moneyman. However, his Polymarket wallet—leaked to the [Financial Times](https://www.ft.com/content/4fccf8b5-5e32-4322-9435-d996a0273dff?syn-25a6b1a6=1)—is most active between 10 am - 2 pm. The possible locations matching this sleep cycle are Hawaii, Fiji, Alaska, US West Coast, US Southwest, New Zealand and Tahiti.

Why is he trading at these hours? Two plausible reason: 1) The quality of the dataset is bad because he makes a few huge trades instead of many small ones 2) Cottrell is a [frontman for Billionaire Tony Bloom](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/jan/23/billionaire-gambler-tony-bloom-denies-owing-millions-george-cottrell)—who runs a quantitative sports gambling syndicate unconstrained by the human need for sleep.

Verdict: 0/10. Dataset quality too poor, inconclusive results.

Alleged Chinese arbitrage trader used as a success story by various grifters to sell Polymarket courses. However, his bedtime of UTC 16:00 - 21:00 lands squarely on 12am - 6am in China, making his location highly possible, even if his trading strategy is difficult to emulate.

**Conclusion:** As you can probably tell, this method is implausible at best. It works if your quarry trades at a high volume, does not automate their work, does not move countries and takes care to go to bed consistently because he/she believes in self-care.

Unfortunately, these qualities are short supply amongst people who use crypto to gamble on airstrikes. You’ll also have a hard time convincing readers to get on board with your conclusions because the counterargument is always ‘what if they’re a nightowl?’.
